WHO WE ARE & WHY WE STARTED

Students with a purpose. Problem-solvers by choice.

DevSolve Community is a student-led technology and problem-solving club founded by Hadya Nuru and Yeabsira Biruh, two Grade 11 Natural Science students. We believe that students should not only learn about the world around them, but also take part in improving it.

We started this club because we believe that many challenges in our school deserve attention, creative thinking, and practical solutions. We wanted to create a space where students can share ideas, work as a team, learn new skills, and turn problems into opportunities for positive change.

🎯

Our Mission

To identify real problems in our school, listen to the people affected by them, and work collaboratively to develop practical, creative, and affordable solutions using technology, critical thinking, and teamwork.

🌍

Our Vision

To build a school community where students become active problem-solvers, use their knowledge to serve others, and create lasting positive change. We hope DevSolve will inspire more students to believe that meaningful change can begin with them.

💡

Our Motive

We do not want to be students who only recognize problems and wait for someone else to solve them. We want to ask questions, understand challenges, explore ideas, and take action. We believe that even a small solution can make a real difference when it addresses a genuine need.
